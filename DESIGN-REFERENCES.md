# Manufacturer manual design study

Reviewed 20 September 2026. These observations concern the rendered pages listed below, not every publication from each manufacturer. Page numbers are physical PDF page numbers, starting at one. No manufacturer artwork, text, or logos are bundled in the sample.

## DJI Mini 4 Pro

Source: [DJI Mini 4 Pro user manual](https://dl.djicdn.com/downloads/DJI_Mini_4_Pro/20240115/DJI_Mini_4_Pro_User_Manual_EN.pdf), pages 11 and 15.

Page 11 places mechanical drawings directly after the relevant actions. Blue arrows communicate movement, while a separately ruled notice area groups handling information. Page 15 uses two equipment views with numbered callouts and a two-column legend below. Running product identification and page numbers remain quiet but consistent.

Applied principle: annotate equipment precisely and keep explanations close to the feature or action. Use an accent for a specific information role. Do not copy product geometry, handling claims, or branded artwork into an unrelated manual.

## Tektronix TBS2000B

Source: [TBS2000B Series Oscilloscopes User Manual](https://download.tek.com/manual/TBS2000B-User-Manual-077152501.pdf), pages 28 and 36.

Page 28 uses a consistent running section title and cyan rule, with front/rear equipment drawings placed beneath the power sequence. Page 36 arranges procedure text beside enlarged connection details and numbered visual cues. Each illustration answers where and how the action is performed.

Applied principle: pair concise actions with sufficiently large diagrams; use a stable grid across sections; keep navigation separate from the main instruction. The sample does not reproduce electrical procedures or imply the fictional equipment has real safety ratings.

## Sony help guide

Source: [1000X THE COLLEXION help guide](https://helpguide.sony.net/mdr/2998/v1/en/print.pdf), pages 7 and 30.

Page 30 uses a prominent step number, an illustrated connection, enlarged connector details, and the expected indicator behavior. Page 7, which describes software functions, uses a different information pattern: grouped feature headings and short explanations under consistent publication identification. The page's layout follows its content role.

Applied principle: use a recognizable hierarchy and match composition to the task. Do not copy the site's web-export artifacts or assume a single page pattern is ideal for every manual section.

## The change to Plain Docs

The first skill treated professional appearance mainly as clean typography and the absence of unnecessary decoration. The user's review showed that this did not meet the intended design standard. The revised skill treats page architecture, original technical figures, navigation, and typography as planned parts of production, while retaining text-integrity checks.

The new sample uses an original fictional instrument, not a lookalike of any source device. It has a document opening with linked contents, an annotated overview, a connection diagram, a schematic interface, export guidance, specifications, and Chinese quick-reference pages. Its shared grid and palette unite the page types without repeating the same paragraph-and-table arrangement everywhere.

This is an application of observed design principles, not proof of equivalent usability or a claim of manufacturer endorsement. It has not been tested with real equipment or in a reader study.
