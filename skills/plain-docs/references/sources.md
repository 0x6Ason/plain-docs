# Sources

The rules in this skill come from these documents. Standards are paywalled;
the links go to their official catalogue pages.

## Instructions and safety information

- IEC/IEEE 82079-1:2019, *Preparation of information for use (instructions
  for use) of products — Part 1: Principles and general requirements*.
  <https://www.iso.org/standard/71620.html>
- ANSI Z535.6-2023, *Product Safety Information in Product Manuals,
  Instructions, and Other Collateral Materials*. Overview:
  <https://blog.ansi.org/ansi-z535-6-2023-product-safety-information>
- ANSI Z535.1, *Safety Colors* (harmonised with ISO 3864-4); signal-word
  colours referenced by PANTONE values.
  <https://blog.ansi.org/ansi-z535-1-2022-safety-colors-standard/>
- ANSI Z535.4-2023, signal words in sans-serif capitals; NOTICE in italic.
  <https://blog.ansi.org/ansi-z535-4-2023-product-safety-sign-or-label/>
- ISO 3864-2, *Graphical symbols — Safety colours and safety signs — Design
  principles for product safety labels*.

## Writing

- Microsoft Writing Style Guide: procedures, formatting UI text, global
  communications. <https://learn.microsoft.com/style-guide/welcome/>
- Microsoft localization style guides, one per language.
  <https://learn.microsoft.com/globalization/reference/microsoft-style-guides>
- Wikipedia, *Signs of AI writing* (WikiProject AI Cleanup): a field guide to
  patterns of generated prose, and a reminder that they are symptoms.
  <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing>

## Typography and layout

- Matthew Butterick, *Practical Typography*: body text size, line spacing,
  45–90 characters per line. <https://practicaltypography.com/>
- Robert Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style*: measure, hanging
  numbers, typographic hierarchy without decoration.
- Josef Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems in Graphic Design*: column grids and
  consistent alignment across page types.
- Markus Kohm, *KOMA-Script* documentation: the base classes and running
  heads. <https://ctan.org/pkg/koma-script>
- Robert Schlicht, *microtype*: protrusion and font expansion.
  <https://ctan.org/pkg/microtype>
- W3C, *Requirements for Chinese Text Layout* and *Requirements for Japanese
  Text Layout*. <https://www.w3.org/TR/clreq/>, <https://www.w3.org/TR/jlreq/>

## Colour and accessibility

- Masataka Okabe and Kei Ito, *Color Universal Design*: the categorical
  palette used for data. <https://jfly.uni-koeln.de/color/>
- W3C, WCAG 2.2: contrast minimum 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and
  graphical objects; use of colour. <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>
- LaTeX Project, tagged PDF status and `\DocumentMetadata`.
  <https://latex3.github.io/tagging-project/>
